# Phase15 C07 - measured book folding

An original experimental Expert → Menu → Grid crafts product. A heart is generated from an implicit curve; text/date uses the existing pinned OFL Noto outlines, contextual Arabic shaping and bidirectional runs. No competitor patterns or photographs. Separate source bands use fixed adjacent leaves rather than filling the gaps. Each leaf is two page faces; the guide identifies both the physical leaf and its odd page face. The count is centered in the available book, with no invented extra leaves.

`iprasm.book-fold/1` / `bounded-corner-reflection/1` binds source bits, physical dimensions, paper thickness, exact ordered leaf marks, crease endpoints and reflected corners. Validation regenerates everything and rejects tampering. Row pitch is at least1mm, nominal fan pitch at least twice the paper thickness, at most8 alternate leaves per column, and the actual book must have enough leaves. This is a conservative planning constraint, not a proof of a finished book's opening angle or paper behavior.

Coordinates use the page's top-left origin, spine on the left. For a top outer-edge mark A=(W,a), put the second mark B=(W-d,0), d=min(a,(W-spine clearance)/2). Reflect the outer top corner across AB. The lower corner uses its bottom-edge counterpart. Every reflected corner stays inside the page and on its side of the visible band. Folds may have angles other than45degrees: a fixed45degree rule would cross the spine for these dimensions. Blank columns use a vertical crease at W-inset and fold the outside strip inward. No cutting. Multiple bands alternate on neighboring leaves; they do not all occupy one impossible leaf.

The preview shows the actual exposed bands at a nominal fan width, including the alternating thin leaf stripes. It is explicitly a schematic, not a finished-book photograph or a source-art overlay. The actual fan, spine, folds, visibility and paper durability require OWNER/O06 trials using expendable paper. No physical acceptance is claimed.
