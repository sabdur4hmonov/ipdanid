# Phase15 C09 - original measured solid models

C09a core and C09b reachable Expert UI/player/physical PDF/rotatable geometry are implemented and software-tested. Actual printer, material, adhesive, rod and phone acceptance remains OWNER. See the executed evidence below; no physical acceptance is inferred.

Three original procedural sources, separate from the standard nail/thread generator:

- Frame: four individually closed45degree mitred rails around the measured rectangular base. The opening is board width/height plus the declared total clearance. Wall and thickness are explicit mm. Four glued butt/mitre seams, no unimplemented snap joints or load-bearing claim. The default500mm base gives500.5mm opening and524.5mm outer rails; this exceeds most small printer beds. Export is one rail at a time, not a falsely unioned mesh of touching rails. The owner must check the actual printer/CNC bed and material/adhesive/fit.
- Layered spindle sculpture: original radius profile `R*(.45+.55*sin(pi*(i+.5)/layers))`,64-sided outer rings and32-sided alignment bores, each layer at its exact ordered Z. Bore radius is adjusted so its minimum across flats equals the requested bore diameter. Output parts are individually closed solid layers, not a fake vase or liquid container. Source diameter is an envelope; the actual generated layer dimensions are available from mesh bounds. Rod fit, glue and physical assembly remain OWNER.
- Lettering: pinned OFL font outlines and the existing contextual/bidirectional shaper, including Latin/Cyrillic/Arabic/mixed text. Original outlines are unioned before extrusion, preserving real counters. Disconnected dots/letters remain separate parts with their exact placement; no invented supports or joining bars. The measured template will locate those parts. Thin physical features must be trialed.

`iprasm.solid-plan/1` / `original-extrusions/1` binds canonical source, exact millimetre polygons, per-part Z/height/volume, optional font/run proof and access policy. Imports will regenerate and compare the complete plan; no imported arbitrary mesh or executable SVG. Current pure exports derive individual closed binary STL and physical current-part SVG/50mm calibration from that plan. STL itself is unitless; the header says mm, and import must use mm. It contains one part, flattened to print-bed Z0. XY remains in assembly coordinates; center the part in the slicer.

Extrusion uses a constrained triangulator with exact boundary walls, consistent top/bottom winding, area equality, nonzero faces and positive volume. Every edge must occur exactly twice with opposing directions. Reversed/missing/degenerate faces are refused. Bounds: maximum40,000triangles in one plan,64parts,15,000input outline vertices, bounded parameters and2MB validated plan. The only generated planar inputs are original simple rail/ring geometry or unioned font polygons; a topological edge check by itself is not advertised as a general arbitrary-mesh self-intersection solver.

Pinned runtime dependencies: poly2tri1.5.0 (BSD-3-Clause), polygon-clipping0.15.7 (MIT), splaytree3.2.3 (MIT), robust-predicates3.0.3 (compatible Unlicense/public-domain dedication, not MIT). Full notices are shipped under public/licenses and precached. Earcut was inspected but not installed; no copied library code or 3D assets. Sources: [poly2tri constraints/API/license](https://github.com/r3mi/poly2tri.js), [polygon-clipping union/API/license](https://github.com/mfogel/polygon-clipping). These are geometry infrastructure; the product dimensions, models and assembly contracts are our own.
