# C21 account erasure checkpoint — 2026-10-24

Account erasure is separate from logout and from deleting a single private photo. Require the current account's password (or its claimed kit code for a kit-only account), an explicit confirmation, and existing authentication/rate limits. Erase all sessions, credentials, ordinary source/guide/recovery data, public gallery content, analytics, inbox and device tokens atomically. Keep an opaque account tombstone so delayed operations cannot resurrect private data. Do not delete another account's rows or revoke a gift already owned by its recipient.

Financial and operational retention is explicit, not a promise to erase every byte immediately. Keep order amounts, payments, refunds, stock/credit ledger and code redemption records under an opaque account ID. Scrub contact addresses for terminal orders. For an unfinished physical order/refund retain only the information needed to fulfill or resolve it (including its approved commissioned artwork); scrub that operational data after the order becomes terminal. Account closure does not cancel charges, initiate a refund or silently destroy an active customer's order. Show the exception before the customer confirms deletion. No legal compliance certification is implied by these application rules.

Local drafts and projects are device-owned and may include work made outside the deleted account. Offer a separate explicit local erase action; account deletion must fence in-flight imports/saves before clearing current-account private caches. Another device learns account revocation on its next request. Already downloaded/offline guides and backups cannot be remotely recalled. Existing SQLite/WAL and external backups require the documented retention/restore policy; logical row deletion is not secure physical disk erasure.
