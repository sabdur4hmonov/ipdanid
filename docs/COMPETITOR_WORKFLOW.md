## Marketplace and conflicting specifications

The public [Walmart QBRIX Solar listing](https://www.walmart.com/ip/660553976) describes **brick art**: 3500 bricks, baseplate, mounting tool, frame/code and digital instructions, with a seller estimate of 6–12 hours. Do not mislabel its piece count as string connections.

A [RingString reseller QBRIX page](https://ringstring.eu/sv/products/qbrix-photo-thread-art-circle-square) mixes a 37×37 cm QBRIX comparison row with 50×50 cm /240-pin /46 cm copy elsewhere. This contradiction prevents assigning 240 pins or 46 cm to that QBRIX SKU. Amazon ASIN B0FR5NFPDX surfaced through a reseller, but [direct Amazon access](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0FR5NFPDX) failed here; Amazon specifications remain unverified. No substitute specs are invented.

As a separate product type, [WAKAI's Thread Ring 88](https://store.wakaisangyo.co.jp/en/products/nkit500) is a fixed geometric design: 20×20 cm board/template/instructions; customer supplies 88 nails and two 20 m thread rolls. It is not a photo generator. This motivates original geometric/outline products with honest kit inventory.

## Ranked ideas for Ip Rasm

1. **Free preview, explicit paid guide unlock:** keep photo trials unlimited; charge one build credit only for an immutable guide. Our default four builds is the owner's policy, not copied competitor quotas. Test retry/double spend/account ownership and permanent offline verification.
2. **Quality before assembly commitment:** normal-size source/target/full-sequence comparison, optional enlarged features and clear connection/metre/time estimates. Marketing screenshots cannot prove likeness; the real QBRIX comparison needs matching owner outputs.
3. **Per-subject review:** people, pets and simple objects appear in public positioning. Support marked important regions and preserve every group member; test unequal subject size. No public evidence establishes competitor three-person quality.
4. **Paced assembly with real saved position:** independently selected guide language, real recorded voices when supplied, explicit confirmed progress, repeat/pin search/offline PDF. Keep announced steps separate from confirmed steps.
5. **Original lettering/geometric outline kits:** exact pin template and one real routed sequence; no reused competitor artwork. Add only after the shared subject solver.

Do not import competitors' universal quality or mistake-forgiveness claims. Their thread stock, step counts, digital quotas and board geometry are SKU-specific. The local owner-photo engine still needs visual/metric acceptance and a physical trial.
