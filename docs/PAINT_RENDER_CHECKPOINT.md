Colour distance, shared boundary and source position have deterministic tie-breaks. Contours lie between unequal regions, rather than being decorative numbered pixel cards. They follow sampled cell edges; smooth tracing is not implemented.

Settings: 8–96 rows/columns, 1–10 mm cell pitch, 2–16 unique declared colours, minimum 1–256 cells bounded by canvas area, maximum 1,500 regions. Gray/colour presets and a custom palette are available. Palette matching and paint quantities in millilitres are not physically calibrated. Coverage is geometric area only.
