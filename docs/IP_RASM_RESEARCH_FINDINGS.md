# Ip Rasm — Instagram and Product Research Handoff

Research date: 4 October 2026. This is a portable record for another Codex account. It does not require the original chat or authenticated browser session.

## Evidence limits

Five Instagram profiles were directly inspected in the user's connected Chrome browser: QBRIX World, RingString Art, Let's String Store, QBRIX India, and Illusions Art. The research covered visible profile information, visible post captions, highlight titles, the QBRIX String Art carousel, and visible comments on two QBRIX posts. It did not inspect every post, comment, highlight story, or private account data.

Official product pages and manufacturer descriptions supplied additional information. Statements about internal algorithms, revenue, market share, or actual fulfillment performance were not independently verified. Code-protected instruction flows were not fully exercised. Competitor claims below are descriptions of their offers, not proof that every feature works as advertised. Metrics and prices are dated snapshots.

## Direct Instagram observations

### QBRIX World

[Profile](https://www.instagram.com/qbrix_world/): approximately 93.2K followers and 1,108 posts at inspection. The profile promoted photo constructors, custom string art, and 3D puzzles. Highlights included Buy Now, 3D Puzzle, Foldbook, and String Art.

[Pinned String Art carousel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DVapbbcjLX4/): kits included an MDF base, nails, hammer, numbering stickers/stencil, thread, holder, hanging hardware, phone stand, and generator code. The demonstrated journey was photo upload, variant selection, emailed instructions, preparing pins, and weaving a continuous thread. Paper/PDF and online voice guidance were described, with voice, pace, and background music choices. Circle/square and heart designs were shown. These screens were marketing images; they were not an authenticated test of the finished generator.

Visible comments asked for the app name/link, website help, and a missing order code. Treat these as friction hypotheses, not measured defect rates. Implement obvious generator entry, order-linked access, code recovery, and support.

[Photo brick post](https://www.instagram.com/qbrix_world/p/DV04Vk9jFzz/): described 3,500 bricks, a baseplate, separator, generator code, instructions, reusable designs, and a box that becomes a wall frame. [Cardboard puzzle post](https://www.instagram.com/qbrix_world/p/DVN72ACjKRe/): described pre-cut numbered parts and roughly one-hour builds.

[BLACKPINK reel](https://www.instagram.com/qbrix_world/reel/Dd3XwMus7Sf/): grid displayed approximately 6.2M views; opened post displayed 64.9K likes. Other visible reels had thousands of views. One visible comment asked how to buy. A viral outlier is not evidence of sustained sales or a proven customer-acquisition strategy.

Visible themes: couples, weddings, pets, celebrities, process/reveal, meaningful gifts, Amazon search, and comment-keyword link requests. Comment automation was not tested.

## Conclusions for implementation

These are recommendations, not verified claims about unmet demand:

- Make pre-purchase photo assessment and material-aware preview genuinely useful.
- Use one sequence for preview, player, and exports; calibrate against physical material.
- Prioritize Uzbek/Russian usability, low-end Android, offline assembly, and recovery.
- Pair the app with reliable kit access, local fulfillment, replacement parts, and support.
- Offer both gift-recipient photo selection and an optional ready-made service.
- Measure confirmed assembly completion, real-result satisfaction, support burden, repeat purchase, and net contribution—not social views alone.
- Launch one validated format first; keep all researched product families in a tracked expansion roadmap.
- Do not call any feature globally unique or claim category leadership based on this sample.

No competitor account, private data, proprietary implementation, or revenue dataset is included in this handoff. The receiving account should revisit official sources for current provider/library details and obtain its own access only when genuinely needed.
