# Phase15 C12a — server-authoritative offers and actual-cost contribution

C12 remains ACTIVE: this checkpoint implements the financial contract and HTTP integration. Customer and Finance Admin controls are the next milestone. No real charge, referral payout or provider message was sent.

Registered/kit accounts can request a five-minute quote for a promo or referral code. The server prices the actual catalog, stock, delivery and current settings; discounts apply only to merchandise and never reduce the merchandise total below one minor unit. Rules have explicit inclusive start/exclusive expiry, percent basis points or fixed minor units, maximum discount, minimum spend, global quota and per-account quota. Strict enums refuse arrays/coercion. Only Finance-authorized staff can change rules or enter actual costs, with revision conflict checks.

An accepted quote binds account, exact cart/unit prices/credits, delivery method, settings revision and effective rule revision. Checkout revalidates all these inside the existing order transaction. Quote binding, redemption, stock and the order commit together; an injected redemption failure rolls everything back. Pending orders reserve quotas until cancellation. Refunds retain usage. Original order retries retain the same body/key and accepted quote; cleanup of an expired consumed quote after thirty days does not break an accepted retry. Existing orders without offers keep their previous behavior.

Referral policy is disabled initially. When enabled, an account may create one public REF code; self-referral and a previous positive paid purchase are refused. Another pending referral reservation also prevents duplicate attribution. Qualified counts require a real positive matching paid payment and eligible current order status. Refunds reverse qualification. Responses expose counts and the public code, not referred buyer identities. Account-ID checks are not identity verification or an anti-sybil guarantee. No automatic referral reward is promised or paid.

Finance reports explicitly cover the latest 200 orders. Unknown actual costs produce a null contribution, not invented zero costs. Six owner-entered minor-unit expenses are materials, packing, fulfillment, provider fees, marketing and referral rewards. Recognized revenue requires the actual matching paid payment and current paid/fulfilled/return-requested status. Contribution subtracts these actual costs from already-discounted revenue, without subtracting the discount twice. Refunded orders have zero recognized revenue and retain actual expenses until explicitly corrected. Cost revisions/history are preserved; duplicate payment callbacks do not duplicate revenue or credits.
