## What the portrait evidence actually establishes

At the inspected320 target, eyes, nostrils, the mouth separation line and hair texture are still present. Corresponding reconstructed crops lose them. Thus complete loss during preprocessing is **not** the demonstrated principal explanation for this frozen input. Segmentation still needs varied-photo checks.

The fresh v12 guide has1,684 connections including70 routing strands,591.181 m estimated thread, max chord repetition4/32 and max nail incidence62/160. At that final state neither distribution ceiling is saturated; requested20,000 is far above actual1,684. Stock1200m is an estimate, not supplied material. Raising those limits or pretending stock capped quality is unjustified.

Eight sweeps stop at compute allowance, with414 chord changes in the last recorded sweep and `plateau=false`. This supports testing further convergence; it does not show that the additional changes improve likeness. New probe materializes the exact original eight-sweep hash without fitting another sweep.

The optical engine already uses multiplicative/exponential compositing and inverse-density updates. Do not diagnose it as an additive subtraction toy. Gain includes physical local contrast and target-dependent overshoot penalties. `candidate()` uses a tone-only approximate count estimate plus neighboring estimate and ±1 proposals, then evaluates complete objective gain. That may slow contrast-oriented convergence; it is not proof of a bad final optimum. Prior full integer searches were already evaluated: consult STRAND_QUALITY_CHECKPOINT before repeating them.

Physical contrast neighborhoods are2.516,5.031,10.063mm. At320 they sample approximately2.006,4.013,8.025 pixels with axis-linear interpolation. This correctly preserves physical distance, but does not necessarily emphasize a narrow nostril/lip gradient. A source-gradient objective is a testable next hypothesis, not an invented anatomy contour.

Each chord crosses large regions. Fitting hair or a dark feature can also darken lighter skin/background. Current spatial weighting is1 outside face,8 inside,16 in eyes/nose/mouth. Old head audit found much of its manually selected upper-head window at weight1; that window also contains white margins and is not a hair segmentation measurement. Raising the entire head weight was tried and rejected because facial regions worsened; face-protected added cycles were inconclusive. Do not repeat either as a new solution.

The statement in an older checkpoint calling the perimeter basis a “known ceiling” is too strong. The experiments show a tradeoff for those searches, not a representability proof. Interior pins might help, but change the physical kit/template and are not the next automatic action.

The bilinear footprint's support changes with computation grid. Finite-width audit code/evidence exists, but no physical optical model is calibrated. A320 or512 visualization is not a physical trial. Keep legacy renderers immutable; test any changed kernel in a separately versioned experiment. No screenshot sharpening/source overlay as a substitute for a better path.

### C. If convergence plateaus: discriminate optimizer failure from objective/basis limits

First run a **known-feasible synthetic reconstruction test**: build a legal constrained path, render it with the exact current operator, use that render as a diagnostic target, and measure the solver against the known feasible reference under equal effort. Account for RGB-byte quantization and actual colors. It is not the owner-photo comparison and cannot establish the owner's target is attainable. Failure despite a known feasible better path isolates a search limitation without guessing the material is incapable.

Then inspect actual source-gradient residuals across nostrils/lip separation, separately from broad skin-tone residuals. A new objective may emphasize real narrow gradient residuals with smooth source-derived weights while preserving eye/skin/jaw guards. Define the objective/version before running. Sparse gain must match independent full recomputation (including fractional physical samples, overlaps and dark/light cases). Change only that objective under the same frozen target/material/layout and equal evaluations; do not simultaneously change crop/resolution/thread.

Evaluate every complete routed sequence. If routing reverses the benefit, a separately bounded degree-preserving move is the next cause-specific test; previous v8 pair removal was undone by routing, so consult QUALITY_CONVERGENCE_CHECKPOINT. Do not automatically rerun its completed probes under a new name.

If an adequately searched source-based objective still exposes a geometry tradeoff, present a separate optional pin-layout/material experiment with exact new coordinates, legal paths, template and orientation. No material or capacity claim without real trial. Do not promise an attainable optimum.

### D. Quality acceptance and fewer actions

Use paired raw source crop, processed target, baseline and candidate at identical display sizes (ordinary160/320 and equal enlarged crops). Evaluate each eye independently, nose shading/nostrils, upper/lower lip boundary, face proportions, jaw, hair, light-skin darkening and crossing texture. Record reviewer decision before winner promotion. A single aggregate “quality score” must not approve output.

Proposed conservative screening tolerances, to be frozen before the next trial: no regional tone or physical-gradient residual worsens by more than max(2% of the baseline metric,0.0001); mean dark excess in the fixed light-skin mask must not rise by more than1/255. These are engineering review screens, not established perceptual thresholds; every visible likeness regression overrides them. Exception requires an explicit documented quality tradeoff, not silent numerical approval.

Only simplify a demonstrably selected strongest complete reference. Measure each proposal against that same fixed reference, never accumulated tolerances. Count every replacement/routing strand, start, knot, color change, thread allowance and action. Report time under unchanged8s/connection+20s/knot+30s/color assumptions as an estimate, not customer measurement. Preserve original and simplified immutable guides with zero transferred progress.

Then expand to at least six authorized/local or appropriately licensed photographs covering individual faces, two people, heart/circle/rectangle, varied lighting/backgrounds and face scale. Include the actual owner pair when its original becomes available; screenshots are not sources. Cases that remain unsuitable get practical warnings/reframing, not guaranteed reconstruction. Acceptance remains per tested case, not universal.
