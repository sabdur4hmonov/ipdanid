# QBRIX and China-market string-art methods - 2026-10-27

## Finding and decision

The public evidence does **not reveal QBRIX's internal solver** or establish a commercial engine that beats our current original-photo control on the same kit. It does establish a practical workflow: suitable source/crop, tonal preparation, alternative previews, sometimes a designer editing the result, then a continuous instruction sequence and controlled physical assembly. These are different kinds of evidence, not proof of a secret universal AI model.

The strongest concrete local finding is a **measured mismatch in the rejected projection adaptation**, not a newly fixed production bug. Its published strand response is1.63-2.07times lower than the response measured from our unchanged physical renderer after matching the solver's500px grid on three procedural single-strand fixtures. Consequently, another uncalibrated algorithm import is not justified. The next technical step is a renderer-consistent forward-operator specification and procedural parity tests, before any further portrait fit. Existing sealed physical/kernel/route experiments remain sealed.

No owner image, kit code, account or contact data was sent to a competitor. No purchase, registration, message, install, code redemption or protected-page bypass occurred. The owner4216/API4656 snapshot and all old guides/APKs remain unchanged.

## Procedural diagnosis: same strand, same coordinate/grid convention

`scripts/diagnose-projection-response.ts` evaluates only three fixed legal single strands on our standard Circle:0→60,0→90,0→120. No photo, optimization, parameter selection or new physical kernel is involved. Every1000pxFloat32image is checked against the retained old renderer. Actual raster coordinates use `classicPins(profile,500)*2`; substituting `classicPins(profile,1000)` would change margins.

The first native1000px reading was retained locally, then a **same-grid** reading was added: exact2×2area-average to500px and bilinear midpoint integration along the original500px chord. Native1000vs500kernel values alone are not a fair same-grid conclusion. Neither measurement alters the displayed image or compositor.

| Strand | Rejected projection kernel prediction | Mean darkness from physical image at solver500grid | Ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
|0→60 |.036 |.0738015264 |2.05004 |
|0→90 |.036 |.0746667652 |2.07408 |
|0→120 |.036 |.0586661341 |1.62961 |

This establishes a specific surrogate/display mismatch in the rejected adaptation. It does **not** establish real-board optical calibration, the original MATLAB implementation's behavior, a bug in current classic3, or the sole cause of the owner's lost nose detail. No published kernel constant was retuned and no portrait fit was repeated. The measurement took129.16ms locally, excluding process startup; it is not a generation benchmark.

The ratios vary by orientation; a single scalar adjustment is not a proven calibration. Intersections and already darkened pixels also require the current image's attenuation, not merely a fixed isolated-line response.

## Concrete technical route

1. **Finish forward-model parity first.** Write a separate fixed specification for a renderer-consistent projection operator: exact same pin coordinates,1000pxcoverage, attenuation,500pxmeasurement, intersections and existing order. Test isolated strands, crossings and short known-feasible continuous prefixes against independent full rendering. Do not scale the failed `.036` kernel until the portrait looks better; that would be the prohibited follow-up sweep.
2. **Check novelty before fitting.** Existing physical-density, source-tone, integer-count, vertex/graph-exchange and route2opt searches already failed their gates. A new projection-domain objective/forward model must be demonstrably distinct; merely naming physical fitting again is not a new method. Matching the forward model is necessary for comparison, not proof of a quality improvement.
3. **Only after a fixed procedural parity pass**, predeclare one bounded research-only original-Circle comparison using the unchanged production-preparation10 input and independent original target;240pins/3000continuouslegaluniquechords/.12mm/.9/minGap25/degree160. Keep all actual face/nose/eyes/mouth/white gates and disclose total runtime. No new fit has yet been authorized by a passing procedural operator test, because that operator does not exist yet.
4. A passing candidate alone justifies unchanged synthetic/Square/Heart/pet/Arabic cases and a new engine identity with matching Worker/player/exports. Customer pan/zoom and optional crop stay under their control. Do not add a designer's drawn source features or a cosmetic overlay as the result.

A licensed commercial/OEM provider is another possible business route, but no provider was purchased/contacted/recommended as a quality winner. Legitimately supplied same-photo QBRIX preview/guide files could later strengthen comparison using [QBRIX_COMPARE_HOWTO_UZ.md](QBRIX_COMPARE_HOWTO_UZ.md); their absence does not block the local procedural work or justify asking for credentials now.
